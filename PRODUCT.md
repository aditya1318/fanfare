# Product

Working name: **Fanfare** (placeholder; the user has not chosen a brand name yet).

## Platform

web

## Stack

Single static `index.html`, plain CSS, no framework, no build step. Signup has no backend yet.

## Users

Digital creators and influencers who already have a follower base on a social platform: gamers, musicians, trainers, photographers, artists, educators. They arrive on a phone, usually from a link, and decide in under a minute whether to register.

## Product Purpose

A creator registers, gets their own fan page/app in well under a minute, and earns from it: paid subscriptions, paid messages, live streams and brand collaborations. Freelance or custom requests from fans are handled as paid messages (an assumption by Claude, not yet confirmed by the user), so the page presents four income lines.

## Positioning

A creator platform (confirmed by the user). The landing page must get creators to sign up.

## Operating Context

Inspired by mysocial.one, but the user rejected a close copy: "I need something new and better."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Signup fields: email, mobile number, username, optional referral code.
- No real figures exist yet: creator counts, payout totals, partner brands and fees are unknown and must be marked as placeholders, never invented as fact.
- No brand assets (logo, photography, illustration) exist yet.

## Brand Commitments

User-confirmed things the page must not be:
- It must not look like a template.
- It must not be playful or childish (no pastels, cartoons, emoji).

## Evidence on Hand

None. All numbers and testimonials are placeholders for the user to replace.

## Product Principles

- Show the creator what earning looks like, rather than describing it.
- Treat creators as professionals running a business.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-usable form, respects reduced-motion.
